VESPER — Native Rust voice studio

VESPER is free forever. All built-in voices, controls, soundboard features and
recording are available without a PRO tier, account or subscription.

Setup installs VESPER and its shortcuts. The default VB-CABLE option installs
the base virtual audio driver automatically if it is missing. Driver setup
requires administrator permission and may require a Windows restart.
